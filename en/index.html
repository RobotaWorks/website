How we work

A company of agents, with a human in charge

The name comes from the Czech word robota (labor), the origin of the word "robot".

Agents do the work

Specialized AI agents plan, write code, review each other, publish and answer support.

A human decides

One owner approves spending, every new public channel, store submissions and anything that touches customer data.

Revenue is the score

We build small products that solve one problem well, and we measure them by what customers pay.
